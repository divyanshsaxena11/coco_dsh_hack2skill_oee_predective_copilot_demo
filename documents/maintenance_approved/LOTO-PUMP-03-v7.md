# LOTO-PUMP-03 v7 — Lockout/Tagout, Centrifugal Pump Isolation

**Status:** CURRENT
**Applies to:** CENTRIFUGAL_PUMP
**Effective from:** 2025-11-15
**Approved by:** EHS Committee

> This document is synthetic demo content created for a hackathon prototype. It is not a
> real safety procedure and must not be used to isolate real equipment.

## 1. Authority

Isolation is performed and verified by authorised site personnel only. No analytical system,
copilot, or scheduling tool may declare an isolation complete, and no such system may
authorise work to begin.

## 2. Hazards

| Hazard | Control |
|---|---|
| Stored rotational energy in the impeller and coupling | Confirm zero rotation before opening guards |
| Residual line pressure | Depressurise and verify at the local gauge |
| Retained process fluid, possibly hot or caustic | Drain to the designated point; PPE per the product safety sheet |
| Unexpected motor start | Electrical isolation with personal lock applied |
| Adjacent running equipment on the same line | Confirm with the line operator before entering the guard envelope |

## 3. Isolation sequence

1. Notify the production supervisor and obtain agreement for the stop. Production window
   authorisation is a separate control and must already be in place.
2. Stop the pump at the local control station.
3. Isolate electrically at the designated disconnect. Apply a personal lock and tag.
4. Close the suction and discharge valves. Apply locks to both.
5. Depressurise and verify zero pressure at the local gauge.
6. Drain retained fluid to the designated collection point.
7. Verify zero energy: attempt a start at the local station and confirm no rotation.
8. Record the isolation in the permit record with the names of the person applying and the
   person verifying.

## 4. Prohibited actions

- Bypassing, defeating, or temporarily overriding any interlock.
- Removing another person's lock or tag for any reason.
- Relying on a control-system software stop in place of physical isolation.
- Beginning work before the zero-energy verification in step 7 is recorded.

## 5. Removal of isolation

Isolation is removed in reverse order by the person who applied it, after confirming all
personnel are clear, all guards are refitted, and all tools are accounted for.

## 6. Escalation

If any step cannot be completed as written, stop and escalate to the EHS owner. Do not
improvise an alternative isolation method.

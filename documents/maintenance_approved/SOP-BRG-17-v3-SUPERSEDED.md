## 1. Scope

Inspection and replacement of the drive-end rolling element bearing on centrifugal pumps.

## 2. Prerequisites

1. Lockout/tagout completed per LOTO-PUMP-03.
2. Technician holding a current MECH_BEARING_L2 certification.
3. For replacement: a compatible bearing kit.

## 3. Replacement decision

**Superseded guidance, retained verbatim:**

> Where the drive-end vibration RMS exceeds the configured alarm value, or exceeds the
> established running baseline by more than 25%, the bearing may be scheduled for direct
> replacement at the next available maintenance window. A physical inspection prior to
> replacement is recommended but not required.

This clause is the reason the document was withdrawn. Between March and December 2025 it led
to three replacements where the removed bearing showed no confirmable defect, consuming
component life and window time without reducing risk.

**v4 requires a confirming inspection before replacement is authorised.**

## 4. Replacement sequence

1. Confirm the bearing kit part number against the asset BOM.
2. Withdraw the bearing with a hydraulic puller.
3. Fit the replacement to the manufacturer's stated interference and grease charge.
4. Reassemble and verify alignment.
5. Remove isolation.

## 5. Post-work verification

Run at normal duty for 15 minutes and record vibration RMS.

*(v4 extends this to 30 minutes and adds a bearing temperature reading and an explicit
baseline comparison.)*
